Conditions Watch

What the reports say about Saturday evening at Chinnaswamy — and why the toss could matter as much as the cricket.

Rain & Dew

Bengaluru is forecast for cloudy skies with spells of rain or thundershowers on match day, and a thunderstorm warning has been issued. If rain interrupts play, overs are reduced and the Duckworth-Lewis-Stern method decides it.

Evening dew is the other big factor — a wet ball is hard to grip, which usually helps the team batting second. Expect the toss-winning captain to think hard about chasing.

The Chinnaswamy Factor

This is a batter's paradise: flat, hard pitch and short boundaries. Chasing sides have won seven of the last ten T20 games here, and the tour's chasing trend is expected to continue.
